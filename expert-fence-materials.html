Fence Materials & Supplies in DC, Maryland and Virginia

Expert Fence has supplied and installed fencing across the Washington DC metropolitan area since 1986. From our yard at 6027 Farrington Avenue in Alexandria, Virginia, we sell contractor-grade fence materials directly to homeowners, builders, property managers and fencing contractors — the same cedar, vinyl, ornamental aluminum and chain link products our own crews install every day.

What we stock

Cedar and pressure-treated fence posts, pickets, rails and panels; vinyl privacy and picket panels; ornamental aluminum and steel sections; chain link fabric, posts and fittings; gate hardware including hinges, latches, drop rods, cane bolts and gate wheels; and decorative post caps in flat, ball, gothic, federal and solar-lit styles.

Custom fabrication

Our Alexandria fabrication shop cuts material to length and builds custom gates, railings, arbors and ornamental ironwork to your drawings. Custom gothic post caps, oversized driveway gates and one-off ornamental panels are all produced in house. Note that cut-to-length and custom-fabricated items are final sale and are not eligible for return or refund.

Delivery and pickup across the DMV

We deliver fence materials throughout Washington DC, Northern Virginia — including Alexandria, Arlington, Fairfax, Falls Church, Springfield, Annandale, McLean, Vienna and Woodbridge — and Maryland communities including Bethesda, Silver Spring, Rockville, Potomac, College Park and Upper Marlboro. Will-call pickup is available at our Alexandria yard during business hours.

Contractor and trade pricing

Fencing contractors, general contractors, HOAs and property management companies qualify for volume trade pricing on all materials and hardware. Select “Commercial / Trade” above to view trade pricing, or contact us for a free estimate on installed fencing.

Free fence estimates

Expert Fence provides free, no-obligation written estimates for fence installation throughout DC, Maryland and Virginia. Call (703) 751-3008 to schedule.
